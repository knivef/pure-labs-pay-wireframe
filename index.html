Questions merchants ask us

Which products can you process?

Nutra and supplement brands can apply today. Peptide and kratom processing is opening soon, and you can join the priority list now. See our acceptable use page for products we don't support.

What do I need to apply?

Your business registration, ID for the owners, a live website with your products and policies, and your last three months of processing statements. For some products we'll also ask for lab certificates.

How is my rate set?

Every rate is custom. We look at your category, your processing history, volume and dispute levels. You see every term in writing before you sign.

How long does approval take?

It depends on how complete your application is. Applications with recent statements and a ready website move fastest.

Do you support subscriptions?

Yes. We support auto-ship and memberships, and we'll help you set up clear billing descriptors and cancellation flows that cut disputes.

Can I switch from my current processor?

Yes, most of our merchants do. Keep your current setup running while we review, then switch once your account is live.
