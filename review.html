How to read it

Mockup is the brand design. Wireframe is the same structure in greyscale with a system font, for talking about layout without talking about colour. Notes adds a numbered yellow tag to every section: click a tag to read the intent, the SEO target and the dev notes. The floating bar in the bottom right switches modes anywhere on the site, and collapses if it gets in the way.
