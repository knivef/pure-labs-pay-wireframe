Nutra merchant account FAQs

Why is nutra considered high risk?

Subscriptions can lead to disputes, health claims draw regulator attention, and most orders are card-not-present. That's why general processors restrict the category. We price and manage for it instead.

Why did my processor close my supplement store?

Usually a terms review, a spike in disputes, or product claims flagged after approval. A processor that underwrites nutra from the start avoids most of these surprises.

Can you process subscription and auto-ship orders?

Yes. We support recurring billing and help you set it up with clear terms and descriptors to keep disputes low.

What do I need to apply?

Business registration, owner ID, a live store with your policies, and your last three months of processing statements. Some products also need lab certificates.

Do you work with brands in Canada?

Yes. We work with nutra brands in both the United States and Canada.

Which products don't you accept?

Products with disease or drug claims, prescription-only ingredients, and ingredients banned by the FDA or Health Canada. Our acceptable use page has the full list. [CONFIRM: partner restricted list]
